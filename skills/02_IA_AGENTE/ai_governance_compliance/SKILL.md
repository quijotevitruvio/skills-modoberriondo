# [ES] Gobernanza y Cumplimiento de IA
# [EN] AI Governance Compliance

[ES] Aseguramiento ético y legal en el despliegue de sistemas de inteligencia artificial.
[EN] Ethical and legal assurance in AI system deployment.


## 🏛️ Regulatory Frameworks
- **EU AI Act Mastery**: Navigating risk classification (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal) and conformity assessments.
- **Global Compliance Standards**: Aligning AI systems with NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and regional data privacy laws (GDPR/APEC).

## 🛡️ Risk & Safety Engineering
- **Bias Detection & Mitigation**: Implementing automated audits to identify and fix algorithmic bias in datasets and model weights.
- **Explainability (XAI)**: Architecting systems that provide human-interpretable reasons for AI-driven decisions.
- **Hallucination Guardrails**: Building verification loops using RAG and symbolic logic to Ensure factual accuracy.

## 📊 Governance Workflows
- **Algorithmic Auditing**: Designing continuous monitoring pipelines to detect model drift and safety regressions.
- **Impact Assessments**: Orchestrating mandatory DPIAs and AI-specific risk assessments for high-impact deployments.
