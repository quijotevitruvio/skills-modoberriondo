## 💬 Conversation Design
- **Prompt Engineering (Chain of Thought)**: Designing multi-step reasoning for accurate answers.
- **Intent Mapping**: Transitioning from intent-based to semantic-search-based routing.
- **Context Windows**: Advanced management of history to prevent hallucination.

## ⚙️ Technical Stack
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Orchestrating the flow from user query to vector retrieval and final synthesis.
- **Guardrails**: Implementing safety layers to block prompt injections and toxic output.
- **Multimodal**: Integrating voice, image, and document processing into chat flows.
