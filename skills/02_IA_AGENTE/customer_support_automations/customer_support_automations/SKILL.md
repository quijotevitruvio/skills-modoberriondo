# Customer Support Automations (Level 99)

Mastering the engineering of high-deflection support systems using intent-based AI and node-based orchestration.

## 🤖 Intent Detection
- **Semantic Routing**: Using LLMs to categorize incoming support queries (Refund, Tracking, Technical) and routing them to the correct workflow.
- **FAQ Knowledge Base RAG**: Powering chatbots with company docs and dynamic order status from the DB.

## ⚙️ Automated Workflows
- **Order Tracking Loop**: Instantly resolving "Where is my order?" requests via WooCommerce API integration.
- **Self-Service Portals**: Building automated flows for returns and address changes without human intervention.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Detecting angry or frustrated customers and escalating them to priority human queues.

## 📊 Deflection Analytics
- **Deflection Rate Mastery**: Tracking how many tickets were resolved entirely by the system.
- **Feedback Loops**: Automated NPS or CSAT collection after every automated interaction.
