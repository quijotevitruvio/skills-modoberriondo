# Free AI Business Stack (Level 99)

The strategic engineering of intelligence systems using cost-zero tools, open-source models, and free-tier APIs.

## 🧠 Local Intelligence (Ollama)
- **DeepSeek / Llama 3 / Mistral**: Running high-performance LLMs locally for data cleaning, summarization, and internal tooling.
- **Privacy First**: Ensuring sensitive business data never leaves the local environment.

## ⚡ Free Tier Orchestration
- **Gemini Free API**: Leveraging massive context windows (1.5 Pro/Flash) for document analysis without cost.
- **Groq API**: High-speed, free-tier tokens for real-time chat and simple intent detection.

## 🛠️ Open Source Ecosystem
- **HuggingFace Libraries**: Using `transformers` and `diffusers` for specialized NLP and Image tasks.
- **Local RAG**: Indexing company docs with `Chromadb` and open-source embeddings (BGE-Small).

## 📦 Business Automation
- **n8n Self-Hosted**: Orchestrating all free AI components into powerful production workflows.
