## 📡 The Three Pillars
- **Distributed Tracing**: Implementing OpenTelemetry to trace requests across microservices and identify bottlenecks.
- **Log Aggregation**: Orchestrating ELK (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana) or Grafana Loki for centralized log analysis.
- **Metrics & Alerting**: Strategic monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana for system-level and business-level KPIs.

## 🛠️ Performance & Anomalies
- **Anomaly Detection**: Using machine learning to identify deviations from "normal" system behavior.
- **Error Budgeting (SLIs/SLOs)**: Defining and monitoring service level objectives.

## 📦 Updatability
Tracks the latest OpenTelemetry and Grafana ecosystem advancements.
