# [EN] Autonomous OSINT Agent

[ES] Investigación automatizada de identidades y activos digitales en red profunda.
[EN] Automated investigation of identities and digital assets across the deep web.


## 🕵️‍♂️ Offensive Intelligence Monitoring
- **Asset Discovery Automation**: Continuous scanning for exposed subdomains, IPs, and cloud buckets associated with your digital perimeter.
- **Leak Detection (Clear & Dark Web)**: Monitoring for leaked database credentials, code commits with secrets, and brand mentions in high-risk environments.
- **Competitive Intelligence**: Tracking competitor infrastructure changes, technology stack migrations, and regional expansion markers via public telemetry.

## 🛠️ Automated Reconnaissance
- **Shodan/Censys Integration**: Orchestrating agents to perform programmatic queries for service fingerprinting and vulnerability mapping.
- **Social Engineering Defense**: Analyzing public employee footprints to identify potential entry points for phishing or human-targeted attacks.
- **Graph-Based OSINT**: Building relationship maps between domains, people, and companies to discover hidden linkages and risks.

## 🚀 Proactive Sovereignty
- **Self-Recon Protocols**: Ensuring that your own technical sovereignty is maintained by finding and fixing misconfigured "Shadow IT" before hackers do.
- **Continuous Intelligence Feed**: Designing agents that provide daily executive summaries of the technical threat landscape affecting your operations.
