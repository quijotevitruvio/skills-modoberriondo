# [ES] Orquestador de CRM y Ventas
# [EN] CRM Orchestrator Sales

[ES] Integración y automatización de procesos de venta y gestión de clientes.
[EN] Sales process and customer management integration and automation.


## 🔗 Sync Architectures
- **HubSpot / Pipedrive Integration**: Syncing form submissions and WooCommerce customers to sales deals using APIs or n8n hooks.
- **Lead Scoring Automation**: Implementing logic to categorize leads based on their interactions *before* they reach the CRM.

## ⚡ Pipeline Velocity
- **Automated Opportunity Creation**: Instantly creating "Deals" or "Opportunities" from web events (purchases, high-value downloads).
- **Notification Loops**: Real-time alerts to sales teams via Slack/WhatsApp for "hot" leads.

## 📊 Data Hygiene
- **Deduplication Engine**: Preventing redundant contacts via email and phone matching logic.
- **Enrichment Workflows**: Using external tools to add company data and social profiles to incoming leads.
