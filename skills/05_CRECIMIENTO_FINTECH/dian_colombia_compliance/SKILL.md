## 🏛️ Regulatory Standards
- **XML UBL 2.1**: Advanced creation of Electronic Invoices (Factura Electrónica), Debit Notes, and Credit Notes in standard UBL format.
- **CUFE & CUDE Generation**: Strategic management of unique codes and QR generation for valid document validation.
- **Radian Events**: Handling receipt, acceptance, and rejection events as per the latest resolution.

## 🔑 Security & Certificates
- **Digital Signatures**: Implementing XAdES-EPES / XAdES-BES digital signatures on the XML payload.
- **WSDL & SOAP Orchestration**: Communicating with DIAN productive and testing environments using the specialized "Web Services" protocols.

## 🛡️ Error Handling (Codigos de Rechazo)
- **Advanced Debugging**: Identifying common integration errors (04, 05, 08) and solving them at the source.
