# [ES] Automatización LegalTech
# [EN] LegalTech Automation

[ES] Sustitución de procesos legales tediosos por lógica digital y contratos inteligentes.
[EN] Replacement of tedious legal processes with digital logic and smart contracts.


## ⚖️ Document Engineering
- **Conditional Contract Logic**: Building templates that generate legal documents based on business rules.
- **Workflow Orchestration**: Automating signing loops (DocuSign, HelloSign) and renewal alerts.

## 🛡️ Compliance & Privacy
- **Privacy Policy Generators**: Dynamic policy updates based on regional regulation (GDPR, CCPA).
- **Automated Data Subject Requests (DSAR)**: Mapping and automating the "right to be forgotten" in SaaS databases.

## 📦 Use Cases
- Automated employee onboarding contracts.
- High-scale Terms of Service (ToS) versioning and user notification.
