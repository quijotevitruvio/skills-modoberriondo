# [ES] Bucles de Marketing Omnicanal
# [EN] Omnichannel Marketing Loops

[ES] Retención y adquisición coordinada en múltiples canales digitales.
[EN] Coordinated retention and acquisition across multiple digital channels.


## 🔄 Retention Architecture
- **Behavioral Triggers**: Launching flows based on specific user actions (Last login > 30 days, Cart Abandoned, High-Value Purchase).
- **Cross-Channel Synchronization**: Ensuring a customer doesn't receive the same message on WhatsApp and Email simultaneously.

## ⚡ Loop Mechanisms
- **The Loyalty Loop**: Automated follow-ups after purchase to drive reviews, referrals, and repeat buys.
- **Win-back Sequences**: Re-engaging "frozen" customers with personalized offers via their preferred channel.
- **Segmentation Engines**: Dynamic grouping of users based on Lifetime Value (LTV) and engagement.

## 📊 Analytics & LTV
- **Attribution Modeling**: Tracking which channel truly drove the conversion.
- **CLV Optimization**: Designing loops specifically to increase the profit per customer over time.
