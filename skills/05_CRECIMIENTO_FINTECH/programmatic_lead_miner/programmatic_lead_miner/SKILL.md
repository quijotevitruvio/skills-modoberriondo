# Programmatic Lead Miner (Level 99)

The advanced engineering of automated systems for discovery, extraction, and enrichment of high-value business leads.

## 🔍 Discovery Engines
- **Dorking Automation**: Using specialized search operators to identify hidden B2B contacts and directory pages.
- **Social Graph Mining**: Extracting intent signals from social networks and professional platforms (API-based).
- **Domain Fingerprinting**: Identifying technologies used by target companies to pitch specific solutions.

## 🛠️ Extraction & Enrichment
- **Headless Scraping**: Orchestrating Playwright/Puppeteer clusters to extract data from protected directories.
- **API Enrichment**: Connecting extracted data to tools like Apollo, Clearbit, or LinkedIn API for email and phone verification.
- **Data Deduplication**: Ensuring a clean master list using fuzzy matching and email normalization.

## ⚙️ Workflow Integration
- **CRM Sync**: Directly injecting enriched leads into the sales pipeline.
- **Intent-Based Segmentation**: Categorizing leads based on their current tech stack or public job postings.
