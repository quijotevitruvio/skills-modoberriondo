# Green Software Architect (Level 99)

Mastering the principles of carbon-efficient and sustainable software engineering for minimum environmental impact and maximum infrastructure efficiency.

## 🍃 Carbon Efficiency Principles
- **Carbon Awareness**: Designing software that shifts intensive computations (AI training/Data processing) to times and locations with cleaner energy.
- **Energy Proportionality**: Ensuring resource usage correlates directly with the work done, eliminating "phantom" idle energy consumption.

## ⚙️ Sustainable Infrastructure
- **Rightsizing & Scalability (KEDA)**: Using event-driven autoscaling to drive compute to zero whenever not in use.
- **Thin Clients & Edge Offloading**: Moving computation closer to the source to reduce data transfer energy (network carbon footprint).

## 📊 Measurement & ESG
- **SCI (Software Carbon Intensity)**: Implementing the Green Software Foundation's standard metrics to score and improve software sustainability.
- **Reporting & Compliance**: Preparing technical data for corporate ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) transparency.
