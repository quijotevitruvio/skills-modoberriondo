# [ES] Ingeniería Verde Sostenible
# [EN] Sustainable Green Engineering

[ES] Desarrollo con conciencia climática y eficiencia eléctrica.
[EN] Development with climate awareness and electrical efficiency.


## 🌿 Energy-Efficient Coding
- **Algorithmic Efficiency**: Reducing CPU cycles for high-scale operations to lower data center power consumption.
- **Payload Management**: Minimizing data transfer to reduce network energy overhead.

## ⚡ Technical Optimization
- **Serverless Scaling**: Ensuring resources are used only when needed.
- **Cold Storage Strategies**: Moving inactive data to low-energy storage tiers.

## 📊 Analytics
- **Carbon Tracking**: Estimating the CO2 footprint of application workloads.
