# BioTech & DTx Engineering (Level 99)

Mastering the technical standards and data architectures for Digital Therapeutics (DTx) and biotechnology integration.

## 🧬 Health Data Standards
- **HL7 FHIR Mastery**: Implementing standardized APIs for health data exchange and interoperability.
- **DICOM / PACS Orchestration**: Advanced handling of medical imaging data at scale in cloud environments.

## ⚙️ DTx Development
- **Evidence-based Logic**: Designing software that delivers therapeutic interventions directly to patients with clinical validation.
- **Biometric Integration**: Connecting real-time data from wearables (Apple Health, Glooko, Dexcom) for predictive health analytics.

## 🛡️ Regulatory Hardening
- **HIPAA & GDPR-Health Compliance**: Implementing strict encryption, auditing, and patient consent management for sensitive data.
- **GxP Standards**: Engineering under Good Practice guidelines for pharmaceutical and medical device software.
