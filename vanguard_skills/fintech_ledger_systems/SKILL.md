# FinTech Ledger Systems (Level 99)

Mastering the high-precision engineering of double-entry bookkeeping and transactional integrity for financial platforms.

## ⚖️ Immutable Ledgers
- **Double-Entry Principles**: Ensuring Every debit has a corresponding credit across isolated ledger accounts.
- **Transaction Atomicity**: Leveraging SQL transactions and distributed locks to prevent race conditions in balances.
- **Idempotency**: Designing APIs that ensure a transaction is only processed once despite network retries.

## 📊 Reconcilliations & Audits
- **Bank Reconcilliation**: Automated matching of internal ledger events with external bank statement APIs.
- **Real-time Reporting**: Generating high-concurrency balance sheets and profit/loss statements.

## 🛡️ Security & Compliance
- **PCI-DSS Compliance**: Designing systems that store sensitive data securely.
- **KYC/AML Integration**: Automated identity verification and anti-money laundering checks.
