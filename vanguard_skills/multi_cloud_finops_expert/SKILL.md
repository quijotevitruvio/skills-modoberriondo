# Multi-Cloud FinOps Expert (Level 99)

The advanced orchestration of financial operations and cost optimization across the global cloud ecosystem (AWS, GCP, Azure).

## 📊 Cloud Arbitrage
- **Spot Instance Management**: Leveraging high-performance, low-cost compute with automated failover across clouds.
- **Cross-Cloud Price Matching**: Real-time balancing of GPU and TPU workloads based on the lowest hourly rates.

## ⚙️ Cost-Centric Architectures
- **Serverless Optimization**: Designing for zero-scale and ultra-lean execution to minimize cold-start costs and wastage.
- **Storage Tiering**: Automated migration of massive datasets between hot, cool, and archive storage tiers based on access frequency.

## 🛡️ Governance & Visibility
- **Anomaly Detection**: Implementing AI-driven alerts for unexpected spend spikes and budget overruns.
- **Unit Economics Tracking**: Designing dashboards to correlate cloud costs directly with business KPIs (e.g., Cost per Transaction).
